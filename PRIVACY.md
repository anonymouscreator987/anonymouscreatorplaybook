# Privacy Policy: Wobbly Gags comment helper

_Last updated: 5 October 2026_

"wobbly gags" is a private tool used only by the owner of the Wobbly Gags YouTube channel to manage
comments on that channel's own videos.

**What it accesses:** with the channel owner's permission (Google OAuth, scope
`youtube.force-ssl`), it reads public comments on the channel's own videos and posts replies or
comments as the channel.

**What it stores:** nothing. Comments are read, a reply is written, and nothing is saved, sold or
shared with anyone. The login token is stored privately by the channel owner and never published.

**Other people's data:** it only sees comments people have publicly posted on Wobbly Gags videos.
It does not access any viewer's account, email or private data.

**Use of Google data:** the app's use of information received from Google APIs follows the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements. It uses the YouTube API Services and follows the
[YouTube Terms of Service](https://www.youtube.com/t/terms) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Removing access:** the channel owner can revoke access any time at
<https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

**Contact:** through the Wobbly Gags YouTube channel.
